Privacy Policy for Water Reminder

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Water Reminder (“the App”) helps users maintain a regular water-drinking routine by providing periodic notifications on their Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including information that may be processed by third-party advertising services.

2. Information We Handle

The developer does not operate an account system or independently collect or send users’ personal information to a developer-controlled server through the App.

The App schedules local reminders on your device. App preferences and settings, where used, are stored locally on your device.

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google or its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • Device and app information, including device type, operating system, and app interactions;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and related measurement information; and
  • Diagnostics, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information.

The information processed and the purposes for processing can depend on your device, region, consent choices, and the App’s advertising configuration. Google handles information under its own privacy terms.

3. Local Storage and Retention

Reminder preferences and App settings, where applicable, remain on your device and are not sent by the developer to a cloud database. You can generally remove locally stored App data through Android settings or by uninstalling the App; Android backup or device settings may affect this behavior.

Information processed by Google AdMob is handled and retained according to Google’s policies and settings. The developer does not control Google’s retention practices.

4. Notifications and Permissions

The App may request permission to post notifications so it can deliver scheduled reminders. You can manage notification permission in the App, where available, or in your Android device settings. If permission is denied or restricted, reminders may not appear as expected.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial advertisements. Google may process information for ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, advertising personalization.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, the App or Google may present consent choices. Your choices and device or Google ad settings may affect whether ads are personalized. For details about Google’s practices, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising technologies
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended to be used to knowingly collect personal information from children. The developer does not independently request personal information from users through the App. However, the advertising SDK may process device or advertising information as described above.

If the App is made available to children or an audience that includes children, its target-audience declaration, advertising configuration, consent flow, and any required child-directed or age-appropriate treatment must be configured in accordance with Google Play, Google’s Families and AdMob policies, and applicable law. Please contact the developer if you have a question about this section.

7. Data Security

The developer does not maintain a developer-controlled cloud database of users’ personal information through the App. Google is responsible for the security measures it applies to information processed through its services. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can control notification permissions through Android settings. You may also be able to manage advertising and privacy choices through consent prompts, your device’s advertising settings, or Google account settings, depending on your location and device. Some choices may affect the ads shown in the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s features, services, or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
